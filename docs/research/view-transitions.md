# View transitions in the Rebroid console

The Apps gallery and Overview show the same managed app at different levels of detail. We tried an icon glide, then a card expansion with a matching return. Both were removed after visual review. The existing resource navigation indicator marks the active section within an app. [Resource navigation indicator](../../apps/web/src/shared/ui/use-resource-navigation-indicator.ts)

## References

- Android's launcher grows the selected icon into the app surface. Google's Material Motion codelab brings that idea inside an app by connecting a selected email to its detail view through a change in the container's bounds. Joe Schettini's Dribbble study shows the corresponding card-to-detail gesture. Rebroid borrows the local origin and how the surface settles. [Android launcher](https://source.android.com/docs/core/display/synched-app-transitions) · [Material Motion codelab](https://developer.android.com/codelabs/material-motion-android) · [Schettini on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/11035402-Card-transition-animation-List-View-Detail-View)
- Johny vino's Dribbble continuity studies explore which elements carry over and the timing of incoming and outgoing content. Keeping the card text at its normal size makes the visual relationship clear while the surface changes shape. Qonto's transaction side panel and Grafana's log inspector offer a nearby pattern for preserving list context during repeated inspection. That pattern is relevant to Rebroid's Run history. [Continuity studies on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/3233952-Material-Design-Motion-Continuity-Freekit) · [Qonto on Dribbble](https://dribbble.com/shots/4851229-Qonto-Transaction-Side-Panel) · [Grafana log inspector](https://grafana.com/docs/grafana/latest/visualizations/simplified-exploration/logs/view-logs/table/#view-log-details)
- Atlassian recommends brief hover feedback and a single visual focal point. IBM Carbon favors subtle, task-focused motion for frequently used interfaces. These informed the card hover lift and the short, decelerating route effects in the prototype. [Atlassian motion](https://atlassian.design/foundations/motion) · [IBM Carbon motion](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/motion/overview)

## Prototype findings

- The native shared-element opening depended on when Next.js could render both surfaces. In a signed-in test with delayed detail data, the expansion could start after the fetch. Starting the expansion from `Link.onNavigate` gave immediate feedback before the response arrived, but the look of the expanding surface led us to drop it. [Next.js Link activation](https://nextjs.org/docs/app/api-reference/components/link#onnavigate) · [React shared elements](https://react.dev/reference/react/ViewTransition#animating-a-shared-element)
